## What "Verified" Means On This Page

This page does **not** copy the entire third-party directory as fact. A centre appears here only when the earlier editorial pass dated 19 June 2026 recorded direct or strong official NOUN support, such as:

- a direct centre page on `nou.edu.ng`
- a strong official NOUN search result naming the centre
- an official NOUN announcement closely tied to the centre

If a row had only a weak signal, a partial naming match, or only a third-party listing, it was not promoted to the verified list below.

That distinction matters. Many students search for a "full list" and land on recycled directories. This page is meant to be narrower and more trustworthy.

## How Students Usually Use A Study Centre

Students usually search for a study centre because they need to complete a specific task, such as:

- complete or follow up on admission screening
- clarify registration issues
- ask about result or academic-record problems
- understand local exam or student-support instructions
- confirm that a centre is still active before travelling

That does **not** mean every centre previously classified as verified handles every task in the same way. Use the list as a historical starting point, then confirm the current address and exact service through the centre or an official support route. If your issue is results-related, also use [How To Check NOUN Results](/articles/how-to-check-noun-results) and [How To Use NOUN Support Or e-Ticketing For Result Problems](/articles/how-to-use-noun-support-or-e-ticketing-for-result-problems).
